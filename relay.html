A payment isn't a transaction. It's trust. Made instant. Made small enough to happen a thousand times a second, so an agent can ask for exactly what it needs and pay for exactly what it used — nothing invoiced, nothing owed.

We built the rail agents transact on.

Vince is infrastructure for agent-to-agent commerce — authorization, metering, and settlement built to clear in milliseconds, for AI systems that call each other far more often than humans ever will.
